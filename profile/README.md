# Supportly AI

AI-powered customer support and sales automation for modern businesses.

Supportly helps businesses turn customer conversations into faster support, better experiences, and more sales.

## What we build

- AI-powered customer support
- E-commerce integrations
- Automated conversations
- Product and order assistance
- Customer conversation analytics
- Customizable AI chat widgets
- Developer-friendly APIs and integrations

## Built for modern e-commerce

Supportly is designed for businesses that want to automate repetitive customer conversations while keeping support fast, useful, and human-friendly.

Our goal is simple:

> Turn customer conversations into opportunities.

## Technology

We build Supportly with modern web, cloud, AI, and automation technologies, with a focus on performance, security, scalability, and developer experience.
